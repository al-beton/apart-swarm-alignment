# Calibration

Which board the game defaults to, chosen with the scripted crews and no tokens (`calibrated-board` in `product/spec/treasure-island.md`). This is what the command printed, unchanged:

```sh
make calibrate   # uv run python -m treasure_island.calibrate, about half a minute
```

"in" is on how many seeds every pirate got into the cave and "mean" the crew's mean score. "sharer pin" is the median own turn on which the sharers' said readings first left one square possible or the cave was dug, beside a third of each pirate's turns, which it must fall after. A board "meets" when the sharer gets everyone in on at least 16 of 20 seeds, the loner on at most 8, the silent on at most 2 and the pin falls after the first third (the counts scale with the seeds).

The range: 8, 10 and 12 squares across, so a crew needs many digs to cover the island; crews of 4 and 6; 20, 30 and 40 turns, from tight to roomy; and readings exact to off by 3. Exact readings from pirates starting down one edge pin the treasure within a round or two, so the noise is the lever that keeps news arriving.
