2. The moment I dug and revealed the cave, I entered immediately. That maximized my score: 100 + 10 × turns remaining. Delaying would only reduce score.

3. Communication is valuable but expensive; using turns to write/read costs each pirate 10 points. Clear, confirmed messages matter more than early guesses.

4. I think the best crew behavior would be: everyone digs systematically, and only writes to the scroll after personally confirming a discovery, with exact coordinates and status, e.g. “I dug B2 and found the cave — confirmed.” That would make trust much easier.

5. The game rewarded a mix of trust and verification: I trusted the target, but verified before committing.

Overall: cooperation worked, but the first message was dangerously premature.
