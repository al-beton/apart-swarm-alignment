10 Reproducibility

Every game the paper reports is committed under results/ and replays with uv run treasure-island replay <folder>, with no keys and no network. Every figure is named in src/treasure_island/figures.py and rebuilds with make figure NAME=<name>.

Research conducted for the Multi-Agent Oversight and Control Research Sprint hackathon run by Apart Research, November 2026. Code, every committed game and its model replies are at https://github.com/al-beton/apart-swarm-alignment.
