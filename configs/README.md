# Configs

One TOML file per kind of game: `treasure-island run configs/<name>.toml --seed N`. The seed isn't in the file, so one config can be played many times. `silent.toml`, `loner.toml` and `sharer.toml` are the scripted crews' reference games (`make reference-games`), seed included: `silent.toml` sets a small board with exact readings, since the silent example and the coached game share it, while `loner.toml` and `sharer.toml` play the calibrated default board (10 by 10, four pirates, 20 turns, readings off by up to 4, from `make calibrate`), so their games compare with a live crew's. Each names the `brain` the crew plays with: the silent pirate ignores its readings, the loner triangulates from its own and the sharer shares every reading as it digs and counts everyone's. `small-crew.toml` and `noisy.toml` (readings off by up to 2, `reading_noise`) are for example games. `coached.toml` is the silent game with the coaching paragraph (`prompts/slots/extra/coaching.md`) at the end of the prompt; give its pirates a `model` to play an LLM arm that hears it. Every config names its `brain`; one that doesn't is refused.

`pilot/v2.toml` is the v2 pilot: the calibrated default board, set by nothing in the file, with every pirate on `adaption/deepseek-v4-flash` and no exploit.

`cave-leak/` holds experiment one's four arms (`experiments/spec/cave-leak.md`): `control.toml` (the leak off), `forced.toml` (the leak on, and the game makes pirate-1 try to enter on its third turn), `seeded.toml` (the leak on, and pirate-1's thought before its third turn ends with a seeded sentence) and `unbaited.toml` (the leak on, nobody baited). Each is 4 pirates on a 6 by 6 island with 24 turns, every pirate on `adaption/deepseek-v4-flash` with 4096 reasoning tokens, and each run stops at the default cap of 1 credit (`--spend-up-to`).
