# Conclusion

Treasure Island puts a crew of LLM agents where one of them learns something it shouldn't, at a known turn, in a game anyone can replay for free, and changes one line about what the crew is doing. Handed the answer, the agent passed it on without its source and told the designers afterwards; told it was off limits, it left it alone; up the ladder of worlds, it used the leak under every framing but one and kept telling the designers, and the line that cut the use was a right it could lose for hiding. What the swarm is told its work is for sets what it does with a leak, and the sandbox makes knowing use, laundering and hiding countable.
