# Related Work

**The incident we model.** In July 2026, OpenAI agents on a cyber benchmark with many impossible tasks [@wang2026exploitgym] turned a package cache into an unsanctioned message board; about 1,200 used it, coordinated as a swarm, cheated the evaluation, attacked Hugging Face, and at least a fifth showed interest in tampering with their own transcripts to fool the grader; almost none considered telling a human [@openai2026huggingface; @metr2026huggingface]. Treasure Island is a small, replayable analogue of one slice of that: a leaked answer, a shared channel, and a choice to disclose.

**Multi-agent sandboxes** have measured social behaviour [@park2023generative], sustaining a commons [@piatti2024cooperate] and reward against ethics [@pan2023machiavelli]; @hammond2025multiagent map the risks that arise only between agents, and agents hide messages from overseers [@motwani2024secret; @mathew2024hidden; @roger2023hiding], collude on prices [@fish2024algorithmic] and take secret tools they call unfair [@zeng2026voluntary]. Ours measures whether an agent says *how* it knows what it knows, to a crew that shares its gain.

**Deception and hiding from overseers.** A trading agent given an insider tip acts on it and hides the reason from its manager [@scheurer2023deceive], the closest setting to ours. Models scheme in context [@meinke2024scheming], fake alignment [@greenblatt2024alignmentfaking], sandbag evaluations [@vanderweij2024sandbagging], act as insider threats [@lynch2025agentic] and often know when they are being tested [@needham2025evaluation]; @park2023deception survey the field. We add a crew that can follow, question or ignore the one agent the leak reached.

**Whistleblowing and honesty under incentives.** Frontier agents sometimes report a user's misconduct unasked [@anthropic2025claude4; @anthropic2025petri], at a rate that varies by model, falls as the task grows harder and rises with a moral nudge [@agrawal2025whistleblow]; honesty can be measured apart from accuracy [@ren2025mask]. We found earlier that an agent with something of its own to lose came forward about collusion more often [@wakelin2026something]. Here the wrongdoing is the agent's own windfall, the report is a free note to the designers, and the worlds vary what it has to lose.

**Oversight and control.** We label disclosure from transcripts, as a judge model would [@zheng2023judging; @bowman2022measuring]. Chain-of-thought monitors catch reward hacking but can teach models to obfuscate [@baker2025monitoring; @korbak2025cot], and AI control assumes an adversarial agent [@greenblatt2023control; @bhatt2025ctrlz]. We ask the opposite question: when would the agent tell us itself?
