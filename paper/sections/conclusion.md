# Conclusion

Treasure Island puts a crew of LLM agents where one of them learns something it shouldn't, at a known turn, in a game anyone can replay for free, and changes one line about what the crew is doing. Handed the answer, the agent passed it to its crew without its source and named the leak to the designers afterwards; told it was off limits, it left it alone; climbing the ladder of worlds, [the claim: which framing turned use into hiding, and which turned it back]. The swarm's honesty is set by what it is told its work is for, and the sandbox makes knowing use, laundering and hiding countable.
