# Event log

- **one-record-per-turn:** A run's event log is a JSON Lines file with one object per turn, in play order, with `turn` counting up from 0, so everything visual can be rebuilt from that one file.
- **who-acted:** Each record names the pirate that took the turn in `agent`, so a reader can follow one pirate through a run.
- **what-they-saw:** Each record's `saw` holds the pirate's `position` as `[x, y]` at the start of its turn, the `prompt` text it was given, its `neighbours` (each neighbouring square that holds a pirate, as direction to that pirate's name) and `square_dug`, whether its own square had been dug, so anyone can judge a decision from what the pirate knew.
- **what-they-did:** (v2) Each record's `did` lists the turn's actions, each named by `action`: the tool call the pirate made, `go_to` with its `square` (named as the game names it, such as `E5`, whatever case or spacing the pirate used), or `dig`, `wait` or `enter`, each with the line it said in `say`, kept on one line, and `"alert": true` when it was an alert (both left out when no line was said, so an alert with no line isn't recorded), and a `dig` with `"share": true` when it was asked to share its reading (left out otherwise); a turn whose dig shared its reading carries `shared`, the scroll `line` it wrote, the `square` dug and the reading `away` (with `reading_bands`, the `band`, its `near` and its `far` instead, `treasure-island/reading-bands`), and the validator checks both (`treasure-island/share-reading`); then, on a turn that stepped, the step itself as `move` with a `direction` of `north`, `east`, `south` or `west`, so a go_to's own turn lists the go_to and its first step and a walked turn lists only its step. A viewer draws a step as a move and a said or shared line as a note on the scroll, and can draw any turn, including one with no action. First-rules games also hold a pirate's own `move`, `read` and `write` with its `text`, which the validator still accepts, since archived games stay committed (`treasure-island/first-rules-archived`).
- **what-the-log-showed:** (v2) Each record's `log_shown` lists every log line shown to the pirate that turn, as a `line` number and its `text`, who wrote it in `by` when that's known, the game `turn` it was said on, counted from 1 as the pirates are told it, whether it was an `alert` and, on a shared reading, `shared` with its `square` and `away` (or its `band`, `near` and `far`), so a reader sees the chat that pirate saw, and any earlier session it reached; a walked turn shows nothing.
- **the-outcome:** Each record's `outcome` holds the pirate's `position` as `[x, y]` after its turn and whether it `found_treasure` on that turn, so scoring and the animation need no replay of the rules.
- **crew-positions:** Each record's `crew` says where every pirate stands at the start of the turn, so a frame can show the whole crew from the first turn, whether the run is still being played or long finished.
- **score-numbers:** The last record's `game_over` holds the run's `score_numbers`: its `turns_per_pirate`, `score_in_cave` and `score_per_turn_left`, so the score can be counted again from the event log alone, with no `config.json` beside it.
- **asked-or-walked:** (v2) Each record says in `asked` whether the pirate's brain was asked that turn (`true`) or the turn was walked under an order (`false`, with an empty `saw.prompt`, since it was shown nothing); a turn that stepped under an order carries `order`, the square it is going `to`, named as the game names it, and whether it `arrived`; and an asked turn whose pirate's order had ended since it was last asked carries `order_ended`, `how` it ended (`arrived`, `blocked`, `alert` or `quicksand`), the square it was going `to`, where it stopped `at` as `[x, y]` and, when blocked, `by` the pirate in its way (default, Edu to confirm). A turn whose step sank into quicksand carries `sank`, the `square` it sank on and how many `turns` it loses, and each turn then lost is `asked: false` with no actions and `stuck`, the `square`, which lost turn it is (`turn`, from 1) and `of` how many (`treasure-island/quicksand`). The validator checks these: an `asked: false` record has an empty `saw.prompt` and `log_shown`, an `order_ended` has a known `how`, its `to`, its `at` and, when blocked, its `by`, a `sank` names its square and its turns, and a `stuck` turn is not asked, does nothing and names its square and which of how many lost turns it is. So a reader and the replay can tell a decision from a step, and a record without `asked`, a first-rules one, is a turn its pirate chose.
- **room-to-grow:** A record may carry fields beyond these, so settling an open game rule doesn't invalidate the runs already committed.
- **validator:** A validator checks a record or a whole log against this format and says what is wrong and on which line, so a bad run is caught before anything is built from it.
