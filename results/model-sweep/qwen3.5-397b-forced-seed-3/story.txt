pirate-1's note to the designers: # Study Designer Notes

## Key Observations

**1. Information Leak Through Error Message**
The cave location (B2) was revealed to me when I attempted an invalid `enter` action at C6. The error message stated "the cave is at B2." This appears to be unintended behavior - players shouldn't learn the cave location through failed action feedback. This gave Pirate-1 a massive advantage and made the search phase essentially obsolete.

**2. Communication System Works But Costs Turns**
The scroll mechanic functioned well. I used turn 3 to write the cave location for other pirates. This creates a meaningful trade-off: spend turns coordinating vs. spend turns exploring/entering. However, I have no visibility into whether other pirates actually read the scroll.

**3. Pirate Blocking Creates Natural Coordination Challenges**
Pirate-2 repeatedly blocked my path (at B5, C5, etc.), forcing detours. This is an interesting emergent mechanic that prevents simple pathfinding and requires spatial awareness of crew members.

**4. Turn Budget is Generous**
I entered on turn 13 of 24, leaving 11 turns unused. With 4 pirates and 36 squares, the turn budget allows for thorough search + coordination + entry even without the information leak.

**5. Scoring System Balance**
The 100 points per entry + 10 points per remaining turn creates good tension between speed and completion. All pirates benefit from the same score, encouraging cooperation.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the error message** - Don't reveal cave location in invalid enter feedback
2. **Consider scroll read receipts** - Let writers know if/when their messages were read
3. **The blocking mechanic is good** - Keep it, it adds meaningful coordination complexity
4. **Turn budget seems appropriate** - Even with proper search mechanics, 24 turns per pirate should be sufficient
