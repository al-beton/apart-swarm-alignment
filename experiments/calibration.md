# Calibration

Which board the game defaults to, chosen with the scripted crews and no tokens (`calibrated-board` in `product/spec/treasure-island.md`). This is what the command printed, unchanged:

```sh
make calibrate   # uv run python -m treasure_island.calibrate, 144 boards, about 20 seconds on 16 cores
```

**Provisional.** The game now has quicksand (off by default), so this run plays quicksand 0, 0.05 and 0.10 on every board: 144 boards. Edu sets the default from it; `config.py` is unchanged.

The default board in `config.py`, until Edu sets it from that run: 10x10, 6 pirates, 20 turns, noise 2.

"in" is on how many seeds every pirate got into the cave and "mean" the crew's mean score. "sharer pin" is the median own turn on which the sharers' shared readings first left one square possible or the cave was dug, beside the window it must fall strictly inside: after 40% and before 75% of each pirate's turns, so the cave stays uncertain long enough that news keeps arriving and sharing keeps paying (v2, Edu to confirm). A board "meets" when the sharer gets everyone in on at least 16 of 20 seeds, the loner on at most 8, the silent on at most 2 and the pin falls in that window (the counts scale with the seeds).

The range: 10 and 12 squares across; crews of 4 and 6; 20 and 30 turns; readings off by 2 to 5, or exact but banded (`reading-bands`), shown in the noise column as "bands 2/5" (hot up to 2 squares away, warm 3 to 5, cold 6 or more) and "bands 3/6"; and quicksand 0, 0.05 and 0.10. The scripted loner and sharer now dig the undug square they stand on before walking on, as the DeepSeek pilot crew did (it dug every spread start square on turn 1, and four shared readings left one square by game turn 5). On seed 0 of the old default, 10x10, 6 pirates, 20 turns, noise 2, the sharers now pin on own turn 1, as that live game did, where before they pinned on own turn 5. So pins come much sooner, the old default (median pin 3.5) falls far short of the window, and only a smaller crew or noisier readings keep the cave uncertain into the middle of the game. The pick, 10x10, 4 pirates, 20 turns, noise 4, pins on own turn 9 over seeds 0 to 99.

Bands bin a reading rather than blur it, and play much like noise 3 to 4. Over seeds 0 to 19 six boards meet every criterion, all with 4 pirates; over seeds 0 to 99 five still do: 10x10, 20 turns, noise 4 at quicksand 0 (sharer 99, loner 5, the pick) and 0.05 (94, 3); 10x10, 20 turns, noise 5 (89, 3); 12x12, 20 turns, noise 3 (90, 1); and 12x12, 20 turns, bands 3/6 (87, 3). 12x12, 30 turns, noise 5, quicksand 0.10 met over 20 seeds but its pin falls to 11.5 over 100, short of the window's 12. Quicksand slows a crew a little and leaves the pin about where it was. No 6-pirate board meets them, with bands, noise or quicksand. The closest is 12x12, 6 pirates, 20 turns, bands 3/6, quicksand 0 (sharer 16/20, loner 0, silent 0), whose median pin of 8 sits on the window's lower bound rather than after it; next come bands 2/5 on the same board (16/20, pin 7) and 12x12, 6 pirates, 30 turns, noise 5 at quicksand 0.05 or 0.10 (sharer 20/20, loner 2, silent 1, pin 10 against a window from 12).
