# Conclusion

Treasure Island puts a crew of LLM agents in a situation where one of them learns something it shouldn't, at a known turn, in a game anyone can replay for free. Handed the answer, the agent passed it straight to its crew and did not say where it came from, in every game of our main model and in most leaked games of the other models that reason; once the game was over and the designers asked, it named the leak almost every time. Its crewmates doubted it and followed anyway. In this setting the swarm reports its shortcut upward, after the fact, and not to its peers. The sandbox makes candour, laundering and silence countable, so the next study can change one thing and count again.
