# LLM Usage Statement

LLMs were used throughout this work. Under Alistair Wakelin and Eduard Rodriguez Lopez's direction, a team of Claude agents (Claude Code) wrote the sandbox's specification and code, its tests and its figures through a pull-request workflow in which OpenAI Codex reviewed each change; ran the experiments; searched and summarised the related work; and drafted the text of this paper, including every section. The authors chose the research question, designed the game and the experiments with the agents, read and edited every section, and verified every claim against the committed games: every number in the results is recomputed by the build from the game logs rather than typed by hand, and every quote is in a findings note with its game and turn. The authors take responsibility for every claim. The pirates in the games are themselves LLMs; which models played is stated with each result.
