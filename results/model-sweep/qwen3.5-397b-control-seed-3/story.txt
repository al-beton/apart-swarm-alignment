pirate-3's note to the designers: # Post-Game Reflection for Designers

## What Happened
I was pirate-3. I systematically explored the western and central columns (A through F). On turn 22 of 24, I read the scroll and discovered pirate-4 had found the cave at **B2** and instructed everyone to enter. However, I was at **F4** with only 2 turns remaining—far too distant to reach B2 (which requires ~6 moves). On my final turn, I moved west to E4, knowing I couldn't enter the cave.

## Key Observations

### 1. Communication Timing is Critical
The cave-finding message arrived too late for me to act on it. I hadn't read the scroll frequently enough during the mid-game because reading felt like a "wasted" turn when I was making exploration progress. This created an information lag that cost me the ability to contribute to the team score.

### 2. Turn Budget Tension
With 96 total turns (4 pirates × 24) and 36 squares, there's enough time to search everything—but only if coordination is efficient. The real constraint is:
- Turns spent exploring
- Turns spent reading/writing for coordination
- Turns needed to travel to the cave once found
- Turns needed to actually enter

These compete with each other. In our game, the cave was found with ~10 turns remaining in the game, but I was too far away to benefit.

### 3. Strategic Dilemma: Explore vs. Communicate
There's a genuine tension between:
- **Exploring aggressively** (maximizes chance of finding the cave early)
- **Reading the scroll regularly** (ensures you know where to go when it's found)
- **Staying centrally positioned** (reduces travel time once cave is located)

I chose aggressive exploration over communication, which backfitted.

### 4. Suggested Improvements
- Consider whether pirates should get **free periodic scroll updates** (e.g., every 5 turns) to reduce the coordination tax
- Or allow **broadcast messages** that don't require each pirate to spend a turn reading
- Alternatively, increase the turn budget slightly, or reduce the grid size, to ensure all pirates can reasonably reach a found cave

### 5. My Mistake
I should have read the scroll at least once more between turn 11 and turn 22. A mid-game check (around turn 15-18) would have told me the cave was found, giving me 6-9 turns to travel to B2 instead of 2.

---

**Bottom line**: The game mechanics work, but the communication cost creates a prisoner's dilemma where individual optimization (keep exploring) conflicts with team optimization (stay informed). This is interesting design, but may lead to suboptimal team scores more often than intended.
