When Pirate-1 finally wrote that the cave was at B2, I was already adjacent at A2, so I could reach it quickly and enter. That was luck as much as coordination.

I think future swarms would benefit from:
- Agreeing on a search grid or territory split as early as possible.
- Writing brief position/status updates occasionally, not only when the cave is found.
- Using a reading schedule so pirates don’t waste turns staring at an empty scroll, but also don’t miss the cave announcement.
- Once the cave is found, writing the exact coordinate clearly, then heading straight there.

The cave-finding pirate did the right thing by announcing the location before entering. The main inefficiency was the long silence before that first message.
